First. The Wednesday Audience. Leo made France the catechesis. Master image: Notre-Dame—the fire and the rebuild. One word for the whole journey: rebirth. The rebuild, he said, sparked synergy among Church, State, institutions, and civil society. That synergy for the common good should become the norm. The criterion is the dignity of the human person—the same line he took with French authorities and at UNESCO. He linked Notre-Dame Vespers to the stadium vigil with about eighty thousand young people. The Church is not stones or cement. It is people who love Jesus Christ. He pointed to France’s baptismal surge and to Maison Bakhita serving migrants. He recalled the Concorde Mass and Communion everywhere—true reconstruction only God can bring about. In Lourdes he restated that every human life, even in extreme frailty, retains inviolable dignity, and he recalled walking with abuse survivors. He urged the October rosary for peace. In Metz he said Europe was reborn after war on democracy, solidarity, fraternity, and Christian roots—the vital lifeblood for renewing the European project. He closed: let us not hide our faith. Full Bollettino text was still thin at briefing time; Vatican News has the wrap. Why this matters: official France frame for Where Peter Is. Rebirth and public faith are rebuttal feedstock against dying-Church and emptied-roots narratives. Assign a France wrap with the Audience as lede, and refresh when the full text posts.

As of this morning, no confirmed vote result, attendance count, or Holy See statement. Do not declare a removal. Treat Gugerotti-orchestration claims as allegations.
